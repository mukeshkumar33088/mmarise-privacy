Privacy Policy — MMA Rise: Fighter Career Sim

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Android app com.mobile.mmarise, developed by Mukesh Kumar.

The game runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit or store your personal data anywhere, it has no account or sign-in, it contains no advertising, and it does no analytics or tracking of any kind.

The only time the app talks to the internet is when you choose to make an in-app purchase, which is handled by Google Play.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app saves is written to its own private storage on your device and never leaves it:

  • Your careers: up to three saved fighters, with their records, finances, businesses and history.
  • Your settings, such as whether vibration is on.
  • Your purchases: which one-time items you own (for example a Bigger Tank pack), so their effect applies to every career.

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else, and no way for us to recover it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create an account or ask you to sign in.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or any device identifier.
  • It contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • It contains no analytics, crash reporting or telemetry.
  • It does not send your saved games or any other content to us or to anyone else.
  • It does not use cookies or any similar tracking technology.

In-app purchases

The game is free to play and offers optional in-app purchases: energy, in-game cash, permanent max-energy upgrades and a one-time launch pack. None of them is needed to play.

All payments are processed by Google Play. We never see, receive or store your payment details: no card number, no billing address, nothing. Google tells the app only which item was bought. Google Play's own handling of your data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • com.android.vending.BILLING, INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: used only so Google Play Billing can process purchases. No other part of the game uses the network.
  • VIBRATE: short vibrations on button presses and during fights. You can turn them off in the game's settings.

Bug reports

The game's settings have a Report a bug button. It opens your own email app with a message addressed to us and a few lines filled in (the game version, your Android version and the week of your career). Nothing is sent unless you press send yourself. If you do, we receive your email address and whatever you wrote, and use them only to reply and fix the problem. We do not share them or add you to any mailing list.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. It is a sports career simulation featuring mixed martial arts, written for teens and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age, because the app collects no personal data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and the date at the top will change. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect material changes unless the app itself gains a feature that needs them.
